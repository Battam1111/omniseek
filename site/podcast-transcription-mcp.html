OmniSeek

Podcast transcription MCP server: let your AI agent quote what was said, with timestamps

Give your agent a podcast or video link, or just a topic, and it gets a transcript of the part that matters, with timestamps, transcribed locally on your own machine.

Try it

ASK YOUR AGENT
Find a podcast episode on speculative decoding and quote what it says about the draft model, with the timestamp.
WHAT YOUR AGENT CALLS
omniseek_search(query="speculative decoding", sources=["apple_podcasts"])
# picks an episode from the returned feed, then:
omniseek_transcribe(url="https://www.buzzsprout.com/2529712/episodes/18794851-speculative-speculative-decoding.mp3",
                    start="1:00", duration="0:45", language="en")
WHAT CAME BACK
  • 1:00 to 1:45“those older systems used a fast, small draft model to guess a few words ahead and then a massive target model came in”

From a real run on 2026-10-10. The first transcription downloads the speech model (about 900 MB) and took 162 seconds for this 45-second slice on a CPU; transcripts are cached after that. Ask for a slice (start, duration) instead of a whole two-hour episode.

Install

TWO COMMANDS
uv tool install "omniseek[asr]" --with torch --with torchaudio
claude mcp add omniseek -- omniseek
Transcription needs the asr extra and PyTorch. On Linux without an NVIDIA GPU add --torch-backend cpu to the first command, or PyTorch pulls several GB of CUDA libraries. Details and disk sizes: Podcast and video transcription.
